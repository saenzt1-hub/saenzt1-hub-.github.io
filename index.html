Understanding Progressive Web Applications (PWAs)

Progressive Web Applications (PWAs) are modern web applications that provide a usual app-like experience directly in the browser. They are reliable, and engaging, which allows users to access content even when they're offline. PWAs use technologies such as service workers to cache important files and ensure smooth performance regardless of their network conditions.

Another characteristic of PWAs is that they can be installed on a user's device just like your usual mobile application. They can include features such as push notifications, offline functionality, and responsive design, which make them highly accessible across different devices and screen sizes.
